# 每日外刊精读 | We published a new topic page on economic inequality

## 原文 & 翻译

（原文较长，本篇为核心段落节选，完整原文见文末链接）

Public attention to economic inequality has grown a lot over the last twenty years. Alongside, the available data has greatly improved too: estimates now cover many more countries and longer time periods, and new international databases have helped make the data more comparable and much more accessible. Where our view was once limited to individual countries, we can increasingly take a global perspective on inequality.

> 过去二十年间，公众对经济不平等问题的关注度大幅提升。与此同时，可用的相关数据也有了极大改善：如今的估算覆盖了更多国家、更长时间跨度，新的国际数据库也让数据可比性更强、获取门槛低得多。过去我们的视野往往局限于单个国家，现在则越来越能从全球视角看待不平等问题。

At the same time, the wide range of sources and metrics now available can be difficult to navigate. There are many different approaches to measuring inequality, each with its own strengths and weaknesses, and it can be hard to know how they relate — or where to start.

> 但与此同时，如今海量的数据来源和衡量指标反而可能让人摸不着头脑。衡量不平等的方法五花八门，各有优劣，人们很难弄清这些方法之间的关联，也不知道该从何入手。

This page is designed to make the range of inequality data easier to understand. Here you’ll find all our data, charts, and writing on economic inequality, organized around three key international databases: the World Bank’s Poverty and Inequality Platform, the Luxembourg Income Study, and the World Inequality Database.

> 本页面旨在帮大家理清各类不平等数据。你可以在这里找到我们关于经济不平等的全部数据、图表和文章，内容围绕三大核心国际数据库整理：世界银行贫困与不平等平台、卢森堡收入研究数据库、世界不平等数据库。

Taken together, this data shows that inequality in many countries is very high and, in many cases, has been rising. Globally, the gaps between the poorest and richest are extremely large and are compounded by overlapping inequalities in health, education, and many other dimensions.

> 综合这些数据可以看出，许多国家的不平等程度已经很高，而且在不少地方还在持续加剧。全球范围内，最贫困群体与最富裕群体之间的差距极为悬殊，健康、教育等诸多领域的不平等相互叠加，让问题更加严峻。

But it also shows that inequality is not rising everywhere. Global income inequality — measured across the world’s population as a whole — has fallen in recent decades, driven in particular by rapid economic growth in parts of Asia. And within many individual countries, income inequality has fallen in recent decades or remained stable.

> 但数据同时也表明，并非所有地区的不平等都在加剧。近几十年来，以全球总人口为统计口径的全球收入不平等有所下降，这主要得益于亚洲部分地区的经济快速增长。而在许多单个国家内部，收入不平等近几十年要么有所下降，要么保持稳定。

This variation across countries tells us something important that is often missed: high and rising inequality is not inevitable. Through their institutions and policy choices, individual countries can affect the level of inequality among their citizens. Inequality is not purely determined by global forces beyond our control; it is something that we can change.

> 各国之间的这种差异揭示了一个常被忽略的重要事实：不平等程度高、持续加剧并非必然趋势。每个国家都可以通过自身的制度建设和政策选择，影响国内民众的不平等水平。不平等不完全是由我们无法掌控的全球力量决定的，它是可以被改变的。

The World Bank’s Poverty and Inequality Platform (PIP) publishes data for almost all countries in the world, based on a large collection of national survey data. To achieve such wide coverage, it pools data from two kinds of surveys. For high-income countries, the data measures people’s incomes after taxes and benefits. For most low- and middle-income countries, it instead measures their consumption. These two concepts are closely related but not the same.1

> 世界银行贫困与不平等平台（简称PIP）基于海量的全国性调查数据，发布了全球几乎所有国家的相关数据。为了实现如此广泛的覆盖，它整合了两类调查的数据：高收入国家的数据统计的是民众扣除税款、计入福利后的可支配收入；而大多数中低收入国家统计的则是民众的消费支出。这两个概念密切相关，但并不等同。¹

The big strength of the World Bank dataset is the global perspective it provides. It gives us a picture of how much people have to live on all across the world, from the richest to the poorest, and everyone in between.2

> 世界银行这套数据集的最大优势在于它的全球视角。它让我们能够看清全球各地民众的生活用度水平——从最富裕到最贫困，以及中间所有阶层的情况。²

When thinking about inequality, what people often have in mind is the level of inequality within a given country. What the World Bank’s global data shows is that this inequality within individual countries is just one part of the story. In addition, there are also large inequalities between countries: the citizens of some countries are much better off than the citizens of others. Below, we take a look at both kinds of inequality.

> 说起不平等，大家通常想到的是某个国家内部的不平等程度。但世界银行的全球数据告诉我们，单个国家内部的不平等只是问题的一部分。除此之外，国与国之间也存在巨大的不平等：有些国家的民众生活水平远高于其他国家的民众。接下来，我们就来看看这两类不平等。

Measures of inequality try to capture how evenly or unevenly economic resources are spread, or “distributed” across the population.

> 各类不平等衡量指标，旨在反映经济资源在人群中的分配均匀程度。

One commonly used measure is the Gini coefficient. Although popular, a downside of the measure is that it is not very easy to interpret. Often, it’s more intuitive to compare incomes at particular points in the distribution.

> 其中一个常用指标是基尼系数。尽管应用广泛，但它的缺点是不太好理解。通常来说，对比收入分布中特定点位的收入水平会更直观。

That’s the approach taken in the chart below. For six different countries, it shows the ratio between the 90th percentile and the 10th percentile. These are the income levels that mark the thresholds for the richest and poorest tenth of the population, respectively. The 90th percentile is the income that just puts someone inside the top 10%. The 10th percentile is the income that just puts someone inside the bottom 10%.

> 下方图表采用的就是这种方法。它展示了六个不同国家的第90百分位数与第10百分位数的比值。这两个数值分别是人群中最富裕10%和最贫困10%的收入门槛线：第90百分位数指的是刚好能进入前10%的收入水平，第10百分位数则是刚好落入后10%的收入水平。

The ratio of these two numbers — the “P90/P10 ratio” — gives us a measure of inequality that’s easy to interpret. It tells us how many times richer a person just inside the bottom 10% would have to be to just enter the top 10%. The bigger the ratio, the bigger the gap separating the richest and poorest tenth.

> 这两个数值的比值就是「P90/P10比率」，是一种非常好理解的不平等衡量指标。它反映的是，一个刚好处于最贫困10%的人，收入要翻多少倍才能刚好迈入最富裕10%的门槛。比率越高，说明最富10%和最穷10%之间的差距越大。

In the United States, there’s a 7-fold gap between the incomes marking the richest tenth and the poorest tenth. In Germany, the gap is much lower: 4.5-fold. In Brazil, inequality is much higher — more than twice as large as in Germany, with a 10-fold gap.

> 在美国，最富裕10%和最贫困10%的收入门槛相差7倍。德国的差距要小得多，为4.5倍。巴西的不平等程度则高得多——差距是德国的两倍多，达到10倍。

The data for Angola, Vietnam, and Madagascar refers to consumption rather than income.3 Looking within these three countries, we again see inequality varies hugely. The relative gap between the top and bottom tenth in Angola is twice as big as it is in either Madagascar or Vietnam.

> 安哥拉、越南和马达加斯加的数据统计的是消费支出而非收入。³ 观察这三个国家的情况，我们同样能看到不平等程度差异巨大：安哥拉最富10%和最穷10%的相对差距，是马达加斯加或越南的两倍。

These large differences demonstrate a basic, but important insight. Inequality varies widely across countries, even among countries with similar levels of economic development, technology adoption, or exposure to global markets. These factors can all play a role, but they do not wholly determine inequality — far from it.4

> 这些巨大的差异指向一个简单却重要的结论：即便在经济发展水平、技术普及程度或全球市场参与度相近的国家之间，不平等程度也可能相去甚远。这些因素确实会产生影响，但绝非不平等的全部决定因素——甚至远非如此。⁴

The variation we see suggests that inequality is not simply dictated by forces beyond a country’s control. Through the institutions they build and the policies they adopt, countries can take different paths that lead to higher or lower levels of inequality.

> 我们观察到的这种差异表明，不平等并非完全由国家无法掌控的力量决定。通过建设不同的制度、采取不同的政策，各国可以走出不同的发展路径，实现或高或低的不平等水平。

The ratios in the chart above show us the different levels of inequality that exist within these countries. But they miss out on something important: the huge income differences that exist between the countries. Being in the richest or poorest tenth within a country means something very different depending on whether you live in a rich or a poor country.

> 上图中的比率向我们展示了这些国家内部的不平等程度差异，但它们忽略了一个重要事实：国与国之间的收入差距同样巨大。你身处一个国家的最富裕10%还是最贫困10%，其实际含义会因你生活在富国还是穷国而天差地别。

## 生词打卡（5 个）

**1. navigate** /ˈnævɪɡeɪt/

- 释义：v. 应对，处理（复杂困难的情况）
- 例句：At the same time, the wide range of sources and metrics now available can be difficult to navigate.

**2. compound** /kəmˈpaʊnd/

- 释义：v. 使恶化，使加剧，使复杂化
- 例句：Globally, the gaps between the poorest and richest are extremely large and are compounded by overlapping inequalities in health, education, and many other dimensions.

**3. inevitable** /ɪnˈevɪtəbl/

- 释义：adj. 不可避免的，必然发生的
- 例句：This variation across countries tells us something important that is often missed: high and rising inequality is not inevitable.

**4. threshold** /ˈθreʃhəʊld/

- 释义：n. 阈值，临界点，门槛
- 例句：These are the income levels that mark the thresholds for the richest and poorest tenth of the population, respectively.

**5. dictate** /dɪkˈteɪt/

- 释义：v. 决定，支配，控制
- 例句：The variation we see suggests that inequality is not simply dictated by forces beyond a country’s control.

## 长难句拆解

**原句：** This variation across countries tells us something important that is often missed: high and rising inequality is not inevitable.

**拆解：** 主干为主谓双宾结构：This variation tells us something.（主语this variation，谓语tells，间接宾语us，直接宾语something）。修饰成分：1. 介词短语across countries作后置定语修饰主语this variation，表“各国之间的（差异）”；2. 形容词important作后置定语修饰不定代词something（形容词修饰不定代词需后置），表“重要的（事实）”；3. 定语从句that is often missed修饰先行词something，关系代词that在从句中作主语，表“常常被忽视的（事实）”；4. 冒号后的high and rising inequality is not inevitable为同位语从句，解释说明something的具体内容，其中high and rising作前置定语修饰inequality。

**翻译：** 各国之间的这种差异向我们揭示了一个常常被忽视的重要事实：高度且不断加剧的不平等并非不可避免。
